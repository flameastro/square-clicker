# square-clicker

🟥 A simple square click game made in JS

<img src="assets/game.gif">

## How it works
You just need simple to click on the squares that appear on the screen.  
Each time you click, the browser will create another square, and you need to click on all of them.

If the option **Die** is activated and you click outside the square, you lose, and automatically your points go to 0 again.  
The color below the points means the color of the square, you can choose one specific RGB color.  
You also can active the light mode or the dark mode.

All of your data (Die option, Theme/Mode option, points, square color) is saved into [localStorage()](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/javascript/javascript-localstorage/), this is like a cookies to remeber what you did.

Good Game!
